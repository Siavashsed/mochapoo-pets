Veterinarian & Site Author
Dr. Emily Ross
DVM - Veterinarian & Dog Nutrition Specialist

I've been practicing veterinary medicine for 14 years. Doodle mixes - and certain unusual breeds like the Sphynx - became my most common and most fascinating patients. They have genuinely different needs than what generic pet care content describes, and most of that content is written by breeders with a financial stake in what they recommend.

Everything on this site is based on peer-reviewed research and clinical observation - not tradition or marketing. If something doesn't hold up scientifically, I'll say so.
