## QUORA
**Answer this question:** Should I get a Mochapoo or a Labradoodle if I live in an apartment?

As a veterinarian, I see both breeds regularly, and for apartment living my answer is almost always the same: the Mochapoo, unless you can commit to serious daily exercise.

The real difference between these two isn't coat or personality. It's size and energy, and how well those match the space you live in.

A Mochapoo (a cockapoo and poodle mix, usually small) is well suited to a small, quiet, mostly indoor life. It needs daily walks and play, but its needs fit comfortably inside an apartment routine.

A Labradoodle is a much larger, higher-energy dog. Many reach 50 to 65 pounds or more. It can live in an apartment, but only if you reliably give it a long, active hour or more every single day, and ideally have access to a yard or a nearby park. Without that outlet, I see the same problems again and again: chewing, barking, anxiety and, in a few cases, surgery. One of my clients in a 640 square foot condo came in after their 62-pound Labradoodle ate a couch cushion.

So ask yourself honestly:

1. How much space do I have, and is there outdoor space nearby?
2. Can I give a dog a spare hour of real exercise daily, including bad-weather days?
3. Is my home mostly quiet and indoors, or am I out a lot?

If the answers point to small, quiet and indoors, choose the Mochapoo. If you have a yard and the time, the Labradoodle can be a wonderful companion.
